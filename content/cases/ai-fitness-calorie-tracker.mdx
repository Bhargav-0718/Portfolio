## The Brief

Manual calorie-tracking apps assume you can weigh your food. Indian users eating
home-cooked meals such as roti, sabzi and dal realistically can't, which breaks
the whole approach. I framed the problem as effortless tracking for home-cooked diets.

## The Investigation

I scoped an AI-first MVP around meal recognition (photo to calories and macros),
a dashboard, and Google Fit sync, prioritised by impact versus effort, with Daily
Active Users as the North Star metric. I built a working prototype and outlined
a free-to-premium go-to-market plan.
